FAQ

Common questions

What areas do you cover?

We work across the Sunshine Coast – from Caloundra up to Noosa and out to the hinterland. Not sure if we cover your area? Just ask.

How do quotes work?

Fill in the quote form with a few details and photos of the area. We’ll get in touch to talk it through and arrange a site visit if needed, then send you a written quote.

How long does a job take?

It depends on the size of the job. Smaller jobs like a path or garden bed can take a day or two; bigger jobs like a driveway or pool surround may take a week or more. We’ll give you a timeframe with your quote.

What kind of work do you do?

Concrete slabs and driveways, pool surrounds and coping, tiling, pavers and pathways, fencing, retaining walls, stone cladding, turf and garden landscaping, and gravel and decorative stone areas.
